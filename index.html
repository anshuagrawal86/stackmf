FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Enterprise Questions & Answers

Clear, straightforward answers on Broadcom contract replacement, dual-run SLAs, and pod onboarding.

How does StackMF guarantee zero downtime during a Broadcom CA-7 or Endevor migration?

We employ an automated dual-run shadow methodology. For workload schedulers like CA-7, both engines execute schedules concurrently, with automated reconciliation scripts validating job sequence, trigger fidelity, and completion return codes (RC=0000). Physical cutover occurs only after 30 consecutive days of 100% operational parity.

Can we hire individual senior engineers or only full squads?

Both models are supported. You can embed an individual Senior Mainframe Lead to augment an existing team, or deploy a turnkey cross-functional squad (COBOL + React + Zowe DevOps). All engineers are deployed under our 48-hour onboarding SLA.

Do you require proprietary tooling that locks us into StackMF?

No. Our philosophy is strict allegiance to open enterprise standards. All migrations transition your workloads to industry standards—such as Git, GitLab, IBM Dependency Based Build, Stonebranch, Control-M, or Linux Foundation Zowe. You retain 100% ownership of all code, configuration scripts, and documentation.

What is the typical timeframe for a Broadcom replacement engagement?

Discovery and dependency extraction typically take 2 to 3 weeks. Full schedule parsing and automated conversion run in parallel over 4 to 8 weeks, followed by shadow execution. Most enterprise migrations complete cleanly within 60 to 90 days—well ahead of typical Broadcom renewal deadlines.

How do you ensure data confidentiality and regulatory compliance?

All engagements operate under bilateral NDAs. Our engineers adhere to SOC-2, HIPAA, and banking-grade security protocols, working within your secure bastion hosts, VPNs, and RACF/Top Secret access governance. No customer data leaves your boundary.
